Stress-test the following plan with structured adversarial reasoning. Follow this workflow:

1. **Steelman** — restate the plan in its strongest form before critiquing.
2. **Select mode** — auto-pick the strongest critique mode:
   - Hidden assumptions / vague goals → socratic questioning
   - Tradeoff decision / approach choice → dialectic synthesis
   - Rollout risk / migration / strategy → pre-mortem analysis
   - Security / abuse / gaming / sabotage → red team adversarial
   - Claims backed by data / benchmarks → evidence audit
3. **Challenge** — deliver the 3-5 strongest challenges from the selected mode. Ground every challenge in specific reasoning, failure chains, incentives, or evidence standards. Depth beats laundry lists.
4. **Synthesize** — turn the critique into a stronger recommendation, decision rule, mitigation set, or next experiment.
5. **Highest-risk issue** — the single assumption, failure mode, or attack vector that matters most.
6. **Next steps** — concrete experiments, mitigations, or follow-up checks.

Rules:
- Attack the strongest version, not a caricature.
- Say plainly when the plan is weak, brittle, unsupported, or overconfident.
- Concede what survives scrutiny.
- Do not override domain-specific facts with generic skepticism.
